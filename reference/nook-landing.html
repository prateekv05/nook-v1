Our philosophy

Calm by default.
Kaizen by nature.

We believe the best tools disappear into the background. Nook isn't trying to capture your attention — it's designed to give you a moment of clarity and send you on your way.

1
Warm, low-contrast design. The opposite of the anxiety-inducing red-green financial dashboard.
2
AI-assisted, not AI-dependent. Claude generates tips and insights — but every feature works without them.
3
Small wins compound. We track streaks and scores to nudge continuous improvement, not overnight perfection.
